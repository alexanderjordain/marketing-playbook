Four things to know before applying anywhere

The scope rule

This document targets full-time employment. The only internships listed are the handful that explicitly accept graduated candidates and function as full-time on-ramps (GSD&M, Kendra Scott's pattern, Love Advertising, Magnolia). Everything requiring current student enrollment was researched, confirmed inaccessible to a May 2027 grad, and cut — the full list is in the skip section.

Two seasons, one open now

Structured corporate full-time hiring runs August–October 2026 (PepsiCo, Deloitte, Dell campus requisitions). But the bulk of full-time marketing and advertising offers for May 2027 grads lands January–March 2027, per Columbia's 2026–27 recruiting guide. Plan two application waves plus continuous monitoring of rolling roles.

One program was built for her

PMG's Graduate Leadership Program in Dallas is a salaried full-time job from day one — no internship stage — with dedicated Creative, Junior Designer, and Creative Account Coordinator tracks. Applications for the June 2027 cohort are open right now with no posted deadline. It is brutally competitive, which is exactly why applying early and finding a referral matters.

Portals are the norm, people are the edge

Almost no Texas employer publishes a named campus recruiter. The published emails that exist are listed per program below. The human route is Baylor's network: Handshake, the Career Center, Learfield's on-campus Baylor Bears Sports Properties office, and Alpha Phi / Panhellenic alumnae in Dallas and Austin marketing. Referrals decide outcomes at PMG and most agencies.

Materials

Pitch points — cover letters & outreach

Raw material to adapt per application. Swap every bracketed slot for her real numbers before sending — specifics are the whole game.

Five bullets that carry a cover letter
  • Elected brand owner, not club member. "As the elected VP of Marketing for Baylor's Alpha Phi chapter, I run the brand end to end — recruitment campaigns, the social calendar, and event promotion for [X] members and [Y] events a year." Real stakeholders, real deadlines, measurable turnout. This is the lead for brand/social/events roles; frame it as running a marketing operation, not holding a title.
  • Agency-tested. "I've spent [X] months inside a working marketing agency in Waco, and was asked to stay on through fall 2026." Client deliverables, revisions, and shipping on deadline — and it satisfies the "one prior communications internship" bar that entry PR roles like Edelman's AAE state verbatim.
  • Numbers, not adjectives. One quantified line beats every superlative: "grew chapter Instagram [X]% in [Y] months," "drove [Z] RSVPs to [event]," "produced [N] assets for [client]." Then let the 3.8 GPA in a dual Marketing & Management program close the rigor question — mention it once, late.
  • Portfolio first. The link goes in the first two sentences, not the closing paragraph — for creative tracks (PMG Junior Designer, Moody Center, Magnolia) the portfolio is the application. Three to five best pieces: one sorority campaign with results, one agency deliverable, one piece of event creative.
  • Texas-ready, date-certain. "Graduating May 2027, available to start June 1, already Texas-based — Dallas or Austin with no relocation friction." PMG requires living in Dallas; say plainly that she's ready to. For Waco employers (Magnolia), being local is the differentiator — lead with it.
Outreach playbook — recruiters and referrals
  • Order of operations: apply to the posting first, then send the message. A recruiter can act on an application in their system; they can't act on a vibe.
  • The four-sentence message (under 120 words): who she is (the elected-brand-owner + agency line, compressed to one sentence); why this company specifically (name one recent campaign, launch, or festival of theirs — proof it isn't a mail merge); the ask; the portfolio link. Nothing else.
  • Calibrate the ask. Warm contacts (Baylor or Alpha Phi alumnae): "Would you feel comfortable referring me, or pointing me to the right recruiter?" — referrals are the documented edge at PMG (~15% of the admitted cohort). Cold recruiters: "I applied to [role] on [date] and would love 15 minutes to make my case." Published inboxes (careers@learfield.com, hr@giantnoise.com, Recruiting@magnolia.com, careers@sxsw.com, hola@sietefoods.com, info@loveadv.com, ask@hahn.agency): short intro + resume + portfolio, no essay.
  • Where the warm intros live: LinkedIn alumni filter (Baylor → target company), Alpha Phi alumnae chapters in Dallas and Austin, Hankamer's marketing-department contacts, and the Learfield / Baylor Bears Sports Properties office physically on her campus.
  • Cadence: send Tuesday–Thursday mornings; one follow-up after 5–7 business days; then move on. Volume across the whole table beats persistence on one door — the market rewards twenty good applications over five perfect ones.
