Four things to know before applying anywhere

There are two seasons, and one is open now.

Structured corporate and program hiring runs August–October 2026 (PepsiCo, H-E-B, Academy, American Airlines, Deloitte, Dell). But the bulk of full-time marketing and advertising offers for May 2027 grads actually land January–March 2027, per Columbia's 2026–27 cross-industry recruiting guide. Plan two application waves, not one.

Watch the enrollment trap.

Many "summer 2027" internships require current enrollment, which excludes a May 2027 graduate (Southwest's Campus Reach and the Spurs internship are confirmed no-gos). The workarounds: full-time new-grad programs, internships that explicitly take recent grads (GSD&M, Frost, Love, Kendra Scott's pattern), and fall 2026 / spring 2027 internships completed while still enrolled.

One program was built for her.

PMG's Graduate Leadership Program in Dallas is a salaried full-time job from day one with dedicated Creative, Junior Designer, and Creative Account Coordinator tracks — and applications for the June 2027 cohort are open right now with no posted deadline. It is brutally competitive, which is exactly why applying early and finding a referral matters.

Contacts are mostly portals, so people are the edge.

Almost no Texas employer publishes a named campus recruiter. The published emails that exist are listed per program below. The human route is Baylor's network: Handshake, the Career Center, Hankamer's marketing internship program staff, Learfield's on-campus Baylor Bears Sports Properties team, and Alpha Phi / Panhellenic alumnae in Dallas and Austin marketing.

For her profile: Creative Account Coordinator, Junior Designer, or Media Marketing.
