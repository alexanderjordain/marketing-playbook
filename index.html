Four things to know before applying anywhere

The scope rule

This document targets full-time employment. The only internships listed are the handful that explicitly accept graduated candidates and function as full-time on-ramps (GSD&M, Kendra Scott's pattern, Love Advertising, Magnolia). Everything requiring current student enrollment was researched, confirmed inaccessible to a May 2027 grad, and cut — the full list is in the skip section.

Two seasons, one open now

Structured corporate full-time hiring runs August–October 2026 (PepsiCo, Deloitte, Dell campus requisitions). But the bulk of full-time marketing and advertising offers for May 2027 grads lands January–March 2027, per Columbia's 2026–27 recruiting guide. Plan two application waves plus continuous monitoring of rolling roles.

One program was built for her

PMG's Graduate Leadership Program in Dallas is a salaried full-time job from day one — no internship stage — with dedicated Creative, Junior Designer, and Creative Account Coordinator tracks. Applications for the June 2027 cohort are open right now with no posted deadline. It is brutally competitive, which is exactly why applying early and finding a referral matters.

Portals are the norm, people are the edge

Almost no Texas employer publishes a named campus recruiter. The published emails that exist are listed per program below. The human route is Baylor's network: Handshake, the Career Center, Learfield's on-campus Baylor Bears Sports Properties office, and Alpha Phi / Panhellenic alumnae in Dallas and Austin marketing. Referrals decide outcomes at PMG and most agencies.
